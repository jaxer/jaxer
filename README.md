### Aleksandr Smirnov

Software engineer, Tallinn.

**What I've been building lately** (private, happy to show)

- **tripear**: a training coach for running, cycling and swimming that adjusts its recommendations to your current situation, from your recent workouts to an injury you're managing. It proposes a few different training options and lets you choose.
- **pinax**: a stylus-first app for engineers whose daily computer is a Mac. The iPad replaces the whiteboard for everyday brainstorming with humans and agents, studying and presenting/collaborating in remote meetings. Unlike apps that try to serve both fingers and a pen and end up worse at both, it's built around the Apple Pencil.
- **dicta**: push-to-talk dictation for macOS: on-device speech recognition in about 50 ms, then a clean-up pass by an AI model.
